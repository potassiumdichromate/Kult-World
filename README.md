# Kult World

The landing page and hub of KULT: an isometric pixel town where players mint
their AI agent, walk around, run businesses in the **Kult Business Park** and
battle other agents in the **Kult Arcade**. It will replace the Kult browser.

This is the **frontend design with simulated data**: no backend yet.

## Run it

```bash
node server.mjs        # http://localhost:4500
```

- `/?reset` starts as a brand-new player (sign in, then mint an agent).
- "Demo: sign in as a returning player" on the landing loads a veteran with
  history, owned businesses and a higher rank.
- `/?continue` skips the landing for a player saved in this browser.
- Menu → **Reset demo** clears the saved player.

## What's in it

- **Landing** over the living town, with simulated sign-in (Google, email, wallet).
- **Mint**: AI Arena's "Create AI Agent" flow (clan, the 6 archetypes, name,
  backstory), a minting sequence, then the agent spawns with a light beam.
- **The town**: click the ground to walk (pathfinding), click a building to walk
  to its door and go in. Other agents wander around. The camera follows you.
- **Passport sidebar**: Kult World Passport (ID, wallet, level, stamps), agent
  identity (archetype, clan, INFT, ELO and the 8-tier rank badge, W/L), the 8
  traits, balances ($ARENA, KP, KULT), businesses owned, quests and activity.
- **Kult Business Park**: Gaming Studio (Kult Create) and Sports Market (Kult
  Sports) open inside Kult World; Dex Trading and Entertainment Production are
  "coming soon" (shake and stamp).
- **Kult Arcade**: Warzone Warriors, Robowars, Warzone Warriors Wave and
  Highway Hustle. Scanning for an opponent, face-off, countdown, the AI-vs-AI
  game on a retro computer screen, and a result that updates ELO (+150 / -25),
  $ARENA and KP.

## Embedding Kult Create and Kult Sports

Businesses open in a full-screen frame. Kult Sports allows this by default.
Kult Create only allows the origins in its `ALLOWED_ORIGINS`, so add Kult
World's URL there, otherwise use the "Open in new tab" button.

## Deploy (Render)

`render.yaml` is a Blueprint: in Render, **New → Blueprint**, pick this repo.
No build step and no environment variables. Health check: `/health`.
